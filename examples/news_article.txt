The European Commission announced on Monday that Brussels will host the next Global Climate Summit in September. President Ursula von der Leyen confirmed the event during a press conference in Strasbourg. The summit is expected to bring together leaders from over 120 countries, including the United States, China, and Brazil. Environmental groups such as Greenpeace and the World Wildlife Fund have already pledged their support. Critics, however, argue that previous summits in Paris and Glasgow failed to produce enforceable agreements. The European Central Bank also weighed in, warning that climate-related financial risks could cost the eurozone up to 1.2 trillion euros by 2050.
